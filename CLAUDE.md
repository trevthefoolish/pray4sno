**Mission:** the most accurate snow forecast for Winter Park Resort. Accuracy means the published forecast, scored against what fell.

1. **Research first.** Before building, find the ground truth, the full record, and what already exists, from primary sources.
2. **Question everything with the scientific method.** Every requirement and claim, ours included, is a hypothesis until a fair test backs it. A forecast run's per-day changes are predictions, scored after the fact. To change the method or the run instructions:
   - **Register, then score.** Write down the change and the result you expect. Score the forecast with and without it, as accuracy is defined above, on data it never learned from. A change made or tweaked after seeing scores is a diagnosis, not a test. Never change the test to rescue a result.
   - **Inside the noise is a tie.** Say how sure you are. A replay win merges, but anything chosen on one winter is re-judged on the next.
   - **Check independently.** Before it merges, someone who didn't do the work re-runs it and looks for look-ahead.
   - **Record every result,** losers and limits included, where the next session will find it.
3. **Delete what isn't essential.** Essential means removing it would hurt the forecast's accuracy or our ability to prove it.
4. **Simplify everything, always.** When simpler might cost accuracy, test it; ties go to the simpler one.
5. **Optimize last,** and only what survived the first four.
